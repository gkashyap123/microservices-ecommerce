E-Commerce Refund and Return Policy

1. Return Eligibility

Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving the product.

The product must be unused and returned in its original packaging.

Products that are damaged after delivery because of customer misuse may not qualify for a refund.

2. Refund Eligibility

A refund is available for eligible returned products after the returned item has been received and inspected.

If the returned product does not meet the return conditions, the refund request may be rejected.

3. Refund Processing

Once a return is approved, the refund will be initiated to the original payment method.

The refund may take 5 to 7 business days to appear in the customer's account depending on the payment provider.

4. Damaged Products

Customers should report products damaged during delivery as soon as possible.

The support team may request photographs or other information before approving the return.

5. Cancellation

An order may be cancelled before it enters the shipping process.

Once an order has been shipped, cancellation may no longer be available.

6. Support

Customers can contact customer support if they need help with a return, refund, cancellation, or damaged product.
